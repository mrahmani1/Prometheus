## Major Advanced Implementations Completed

### 1. Advanced Temperature to Radiance Conversion
**Method**: `_temperature_to_radiance()`
**Enhancements**:
- Full Planck function with atmospheric effects
- Emissivity corrections using Kirchhoff's law
- Atmospheric transmittance modeling
- Sensor spectral response functions
- Multi-wavelength support
- Physical constants from CODATA 2018

**Method**: `_calculate_vegetation_emissivity_correction()`
**Enhancements**:
- Comprehensive vegetation indices suite (NDVI, EVI, SAVI, MSAVI, ARVI, GNDVI)
- Physics-based emissivity calculations
- Phenological state assessment
- Canopy structure corrections
- Moisture content effects
- Seasonal and diurnal corrections
- Advanced uncertainty quantification
- Multi-spectral vegetation analysis

### 3. Advanced Spectral Mixing Model
**Method**: `_apply_spectral_mixing_model()`
**Enhancements**:
- Multi-endmember spectral unmixing
- Non-linear mixing with temperature effects
- Intimate mixing using Hapke theory
- Layered mixing for stratified surfaces
- Temperature-dependent emissivity corrections
- Spatial scale corrections
- Viewing geometry corrections
- Comprehensive uncertainty propagation

- Hotspot elevation modeling
- Continental rift analysis
- Isostatic corrections
- Climate and erosion effects
