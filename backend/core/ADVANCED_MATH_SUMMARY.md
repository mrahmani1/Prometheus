### 3. Homological Algebra
- **Tor Groups**: Computation using projective resolutions and Koszul complexes
- **Ext Groups**: Analysis via injective resolutions and Yoneda extensions

- **Primary Obstructions**: Computation using Postnikov towers and k-invariants
- **Secondary Obstructions**: Analysis via Whitehead towers and Toda brackets
- **Massey Products**: Higher-order cohomological operations
- **Extension Problems**: Classification of fibrations and extensions

### 5. Advanced Category Theory
- **Derived Categories**: Triangulated categories with translation functors
- **Tilting Theory**: Tilting objects and derived equivalences
- **Fourier-Mukai Transforms**: Integral transforms and kernels
- **Exceptional Collections**: Mutations and semiorthogonal decompositions
